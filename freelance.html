03 / Project pricing

Quoted to
your scope.

Every project is priced individually. The fee scales with the work involved, the deliverables you need, and your required completion date.

Projects starting at$1,000+USD · Final fee based on project requirements
01

Scope items

The systems, assemblies, and components to be modeled, including their quantities, complexity, and project boundaries.

02

Square footage

The area included in the modeling scope. Specify whether the square footage covers the façade, glazing, building, or another defined area.

03

Level of development / LOD

The required model detail and information, based on how your team will use it. We’ll agree on the LOD before work begins.

04

Model or model + drawings

Choose model-only delivery or a model with drawings. The drawing types, sheet count, file formats, and review requirements shape the quote.

05

Required completion date

Your final deadline, review milestones, and time available to complete the work. Expedited timelines are considered when defining the fee and schedule.

Send your scope, approximate square footage, required LOD, deliverables, and target date. I’ll review the brief and prepare a project-specific quote.
