Every project is priced individually. The fee scales with the work involved, the deliverables you need, and your required completion date.

01

Scope items

The systems, assemblies, and components to be modeled, including their quantities, complexity, and project boundaries.

02

Square footage

The area included in the modeling scope. Specify whether the square footage covers the façade, glazing, building, or another defined area.

03

Level of development / LOD

The required model detail and information, based on how your team will use it. We’ll agree on the LOD before work begins.

04

Model or model + drawings

Choose model-only delivery or a model with drawings. The drawing types, sheet count, file formats, and review requirements shape the quote.

05

Required completion date

Your final deadline, review milestones, and time available to complete the work. Expedited timelines are considered when defining the fee and schedule.

Send your scope, approximate square footage, required LOD, deliverables, and target date. I’ll review the brief and prepare a project-specific quote.

Custom applications

Dynamo & pyRevit

Purpose-built Dynamo graphs and pyRevit tools that simplify repetitive Revit work.

Custom scripts starting at$100+USD · Final fee based on application scope
Explore scripts & pricingClose scripts & pricing+

Pricing depends on the workflow, inputs and outputs, interface, Revit version, and deadline. Applications can include:

01

Project setup, views & sheets

Populate project information from Excel, create plan views and sheets, duplicate elevations, apply templates, and organize view placement.

02

Automated dimensioning

Dimension curtain walls, grids, levels, panels, glass, doors, and embeds in plans, elevations, and sections, using your preferred styles and offsets.

03

Tagging & detail references

Place tags for infills, doors, glass, walls, units, and embeds. Create wall callouts, unit sections, and reference detail bubbles.

04

Model data & Excel workflows

Assign glass marks, component IDs, and system values. Export schedules and glass-pricing data to Excel, or import project information and general notes.

05

Family & parameter management

Batch-load families and nested components, add shared parameters and formulas, and update family information across a selected set of files.

06

View cleanup & graphic standards

Clean elevations and sections, rotate crop regions, standardize level-head placement, control imported CAD layers, and color model elements for review.

Share the repetitive task, required inputs and outputs, Revit version, and target date. I’ll define the application scope, review requirements, and project-specific price.
